# Nine new glyphs, one for every word in the vocabulary

The published glyph set gains nine glyphs, so every word in
`design/vocabulary.md` now stands beside one. Livery keeps its five colours.

- Harness drill (running it): the car in its garage bay, cockpit empty, because
  a drill tests the car, not the driver.
- Exhibition (the race): a calendar whose body is dashed, a race with no date
  on it yet.
- Track card and checkpoint (rules): a card with the track drawn on it, and a
  map pin with the stewards' tick.
- Fuel triad, mileage, lean and rich, racing line and the guzzler (fuel): input
  as a ring with its cached share, a colon and output as a dot; a drop going
  the distance; a small drop and a big one on the fuel axis; the best-value
  frontier; and slipstream's car, dripping fuel.

The dictionary page shows a card for each, with visitor wording and, where the
site shows the idea, a link to it. The docs glyph sheet widens so every id fits
under its glyph.
