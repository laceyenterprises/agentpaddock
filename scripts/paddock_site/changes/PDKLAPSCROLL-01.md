# Scrolling drives the lap on a phone

On a phone, the first scroll down the Learn page drives the sector stepper.
The stepper pins under the nav, centred in the room below it, and the car
follows the real curve of the track through the four sectors while the page
scrolls. A tenth of the run at each end holds the first and last sector. The
Back and Next buttons step aside while scrolling is the control, and the track
hint reads "Scroll to drive the lap".

At the end the stepper lets go and the page scrolls on. The run of scroll it
used is removed and the page is shifted by the same amount, so nothing on
screen moves, and the stepper does not pin again in that page view. A tap or
key on the stepper hands it back to taps where it stands. Wide screens, reduced
motion, a link into the page, a page opened below the stepper and a screen too
short to hold the pinned stepper keep the tap stepper.

The fourth sector's notice and the DNF flag now say what a DNF does: it is not
thrown out, it counts as a fail in the entry's avg pass rate.
