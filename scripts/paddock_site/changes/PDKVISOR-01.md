# The driver in the garage wears a blue visor

The garage draws the chosen entry's car in its livery with the driver in the
cockpit. The helmet was ink with a white visor, so on the dark board the
driver disappeared into the car. The visor is now Lacey blue (`--blue`), which
reads on every livery, Pi's blue included, in both themes. The approved
prototype (`design/web/Garage.dc.html`) and the design system's illustration
rules say the same: on an ink car the helmet itself is blue; on a livery car
the helmet is ink and its visor blue.

The sweep found no other car illustration with the problem. The illustration
boards draw the helmet itself in the accent; the grey helmets on the review
and fuel boards are deliberately the de-emphasised entry.
